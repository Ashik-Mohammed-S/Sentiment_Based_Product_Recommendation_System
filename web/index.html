The method behind the picks

What these numbers mean

Precision@5 counts relevant held-out items among five recommendations. NDCG@5 also rewards putting those items higher. A relevant item has an observed rating of at least four stars.

The evaluation includes new users and unseen test items. Unobserved products are unjudged. A random pair split is an offline check, not evidence of future shopping behavior or an online experiment.

Positive-review percentages use a smoothed estimate, so a single favorable review cannot masquerade as strong evidence. Preferences are kept in this browser’s local storage and can be cleared.
